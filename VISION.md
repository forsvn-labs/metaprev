# metaprev vision

metaprev helps developers, content authors, and coding agents catch broken
share metadata before sharing a page. It inspects the actual page and image,
explains observed compatibility risks, and creates a local repair workspace.
Success means evidence-led fixes and the same findings across CLI, JSON, and
HTML, without uploading site content to a hosted inspection service.

## Scope

Fetch local or deployed HTTP(S) pages; parse Open Graph and X metadata;
validate image facts and fallbacks; show representative platform cards,
crop evidence, themes, and copy-ready repairs. Keep stable issue codes and
exit behavior suitable for CI. Treat page-controlled input as untrusted.

## Non-goals

Exact third-party rendering or cache guarantees, generic SEO copy-length
advice, invented metadata, hosted scanning, analytics, and social-media
management are outside the product. A dedicated brand or landing page is
optional only after sustained demand. Current work lives in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md).
