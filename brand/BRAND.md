# Metaprev brand

The website identity was designed with Opus 5.5 on 24 October 2026. The
site treats a share card as a print proof that you check before publication.
The product remains a FORSVN metadata inspector; cards are representative.

## Implementation sources

- [Website styles](../site/public/styles.css) own the palette, type, spacing,
  controls, card layouts, and responsive rules.
- [Site mark](../site/public/mark.svg) places a card within crop marks.
- [Share artwork](../site/public/og.svg) is the editable source for the committed PNG.
- [Font license](../site/public/fonts/OFL.txt) covers the self-hosted Archivo
  and Geist Mono subsets inherited from the FORSVN design system.

The site uses light paper neutrals, dark text, and registration magenta for
inspection marks and active controls. Platform previews retain their own card
appearance. Missing fields and image failures stay visible; the UI does not
invent a successful inspection. Empty-state data is labeled as an example.
